How I got here

For 18 years, medical imaging taught me how mission-critical systems really work — where uptime isn't a metric, it's a patient waiting on a scan. I led teams, ran EMR and PACS integrations, and learned to stay calm when the stakes were highest. Now I build the infrastructure behind that kind of reliability. Over the past few years I've gone deep into cloud and DevOps — Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, Azure — culminating in a self-built, high-availability Kubernetes cluster I run, break, and rebuild on purpose. I bring a rare combination: the operational discipline of healthcare and the hands-on skills of a cloud engineer. Let's build something that stays up.
